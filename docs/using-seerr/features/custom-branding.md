# Custom Branding

You can make Allseerr look like your own service. All of these are in **Settings &rarr; General**.

## Application Title

The name shown in the top bar, on the sign in page, in page titles, in emails and when the app is installed on a phone or computer. New installs default to **Allseerr**.

## Application Logo

Upload a **square** PNG, SVG or WebP image of up to 2 MB (at least 64 pixels wide). Allseerr uses it in the top bar, on the sign in page, as the browser icon, as the home screen icon and in notifications. From that one image it generates the favicon, the app icons in every size phones and browsers ask for, and the iOS launch screens.

SVG files are checked for unsafe content before they are accepted. **Reset to Default** brings back the Allseerr logo.

## Accent Color

The accent color of the Ambient interface. Users can pick their own accent color in their profile settings. **Reset to Default** brings back the default orange.

## Phones keep the old name and logo

Phones and tablets keep the name and logo the app had **when it was added to the home screen**. After you change the title or logo, remove Allseerr from the home screen and add it to the home screen again to see the change. Let your users know they need to do the same.

## Emails

Emails use your application title. Mail clients load the logo from the server, so they need to be able to reach your **Application URL** to show your logo.
